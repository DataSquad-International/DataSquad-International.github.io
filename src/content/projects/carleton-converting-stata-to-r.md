I helped translate Stata files to legible R files for an Economics professor's senior comps research course.
The translated files consisted of three main course exercises and their respective answer keys. My work
emphasized making the exercise and answer key replications as accessible as possible for novice R users.
Since this professor had never coded in R before, it was important to facilitate their understanding of how
these exercises functioned in R and make them easy to navigate.

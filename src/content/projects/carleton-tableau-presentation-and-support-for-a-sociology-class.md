Task:
Professor Nierobisz wanted to find a more
engaging approach to data for her criminology course. The
data were fascinating - but diving into them with SPSS (a
tool well known to Prof. Nierobisz) was an obstacle that
diverted their attention from the content of the course. In
stepped the DataSquad to support Tableau, a data
visualization software with which Prof. Nierobisz had only
passing familiarity. Yiwen Luo and Paula provided in-class
instruction and follow-up office hours to support Prof
Nierobisz and her students in Tableau and sometimes in the
finer details of working with data. With Tableau students
were empowered to create informative data visualisations.

Goal & Result:
Provide Prof Nierobisz (and her
students) hands-on support so she could stay focused on
course content & not fuss with the inevitable technical
obstacles. The DataSquad also provided a step-to-step
tutorial to help students familiarize themselves with
Tableau and their data. The data that students used to learn
Tableau is also part of the curriculum, so students both
learn the course material and the data visualisation skills
at the same time.

Before writing new lessons, the UC OSPO education group needed to know what already existed. The inventory is a spreadsheet of 55 candidate lessons gathered from Open Source Guides, CodeRefinery, the Carpentries Incubator, TeachingOpenSource.org and lessons the network produced itself.

Each lesson is described with the same 43 fields, borrowing schema.org's learning resource terms (name, description, audience, educational level, time required, license, what it teaches) and adding the group's own: learner category, how relevant it is to an OSPO, the role in an open source project it serves, and what it depends on. Putting lessons from different sources into one shape is what lets them be compared and sorted.

The lessons are sorted into five learner categories: building community, contributing to a project, maintaining and sustaining software, understanding licensing and compliance, and strategic practices and career development. 32 are marked as keep candidates.

The most useful tab is the gap list. Licensing and compliance, a plain introduction for people who have heard of open source but are unsure what it is, security and institutional policy, and governance each show up as topics the group had no lesson for. The licensing gap is now being written, and a work plan for it was on the 2026-09-01 education meeting agenda.

The inventory was assembled in May 2026 for an education group meeting, and it is tracked in the UC-OSPO-Network education repository.

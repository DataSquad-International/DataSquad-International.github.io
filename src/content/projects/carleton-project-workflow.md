For our blogs, we use a workflow that organizes our projects and makes it easier to track everyone's project progress.
This is important for our work as a team because otherwise, all the interesting projects people work on get hidden
among each other. This process breaks blog-post creation into five main stages: archive, delete, draft, proposed, and
published, through which individual project folders move over their lifespan, ensuring that nothing gets lost.
